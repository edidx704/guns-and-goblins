# Divine Enchantment — Mechanics

*Creating Holy Artifacts in Service of Ilpatar*

---

## Overview

Divine Enchantment allows the faithful to create relics — items imbued with sacred power through prayer and sacrifice. Unlike arcane enchantment, this power comes from Ilpatar, the unknowable Creator, and requires no spell colleges or prerequisite chains.

---

## The Core Skill

### Religious Ritual (VH)

**Defaults:** IQ-6

This is the foundational skill for all divine work. It represents the blessed individual's knowledge of proper prayers, rituals, and devotional practices.

A character with **Divinity** (15 pts/level, max 4) may use Religious Ritual to create relics.

---

## How It Works

### Techniques (Blessings)

Each blessing is a **Hard technique** defaulting from Religious Ritual. The default penalty equals the blessing's **prerequisite count** (see Blessings document).

A practitioner can:
- **Attempt any blessing at default** — no need to "learn" it first
- **Improve a technique** by spending character points (max = Religious Ritual skill)

### Time Required

Divine enchantment uses **Quick and Dirty** timing (Magic p. 17):

**1 hour per 100 energy required (round up)**

| Energy | Time |
|--------|------|
| 1-100 | 1 hour |
| 101-200 | 2 hours |
| 201-300 | 3 hours |

### The Ritual

1. **Gather energy** (see Energy Sources below)
2. **Perform the blessing** — prayer, anointing, laying on of hands
3. **Religious Ritual roll** at the end
   - **Success:** Item is blessed
   - **Failure:** Energy wasted, try again
   - **Critical failure:** Energy wasted, may not attempt this blessing again until skill improves

## Energy Sources

### Sacrifice of Religious Materials

The primary method. Candles, incense, prayer papers, holy water, ceremonial oils — properly consecrated supplies.

**$40 worth = 1 energy**

These materials are consumed in the ritual — candles burn, incense smokes, papers are burned or buried.

*Logistics:* A blessing-focused character will want to maintain a supply chest. Replenished at temples, requisitioned from supply depots, traded in villages.

### Fatigue Points

The blessed may use their own FP to power enchantments. Standard FP recovery applies (1 FP per 10 minutes rest).

**1 FP = 1 energy**

### Ceremonial Contribution

Faithful assistants may contribute FP during a ceremony:

| Participant | Contribution |
|-------------|--------------|
| Character with Divinity | Any amount |
| Faithful believer (knows blessing at 15+) | Up to 3 FP |
| Faithful believer (any) | 1 FP |

Maximum contribution from assistants: 100 FP total.

### Personal Sacrifice

Giving of one's own life force:

**1 HP = 2 energy**

This is painful and risky but available in emergencies.

## Cost Modifiers

### Bane (Limiting Enchantment)

Blessings may be restricted to work only against a specific foe. This dramatically reduces energy cost:

| Specificity | Cost Divider |
|-------------|--------------|
| Specific nation, religion, or race | ÷2 |
| Specific creature type or folk of a certain city | ÷3 |
| Specific family | ÷4 |
| One specific individual | ÷10 |

**Example:** Puissance +2 on a tank shell (100 energy) with Bane vs. Moran forces (÷3) = **33 energy**.

### Temporary Enchantment

Items may be blessed for limited uses at reduced cost:

| Uses | Cost Multiplier |
|------|-----------------|
| 1 use | ×15% |
| 2 uses | ×30% |
| 4 uses | ×60% |
| 7+ uses | Exceeds permanent cost |

**Example:** Puissance +1 on a tank shell, vs Moran, single use:
- Base: 250 ÷ 10 (missile) = 25
- Bane (÷3): 8.3
- Temporary (×15%): **~2 energy**

Temporary Enchantment cannot be combined with other blessings except: Bane, Limit, Name, Password.

### Missiles

Blessings on ammunition (arrows, bullets, shells) cost **÷10** normal energy.

Blessings on missile weapons (bows, guns) cost **×2** normal energy.

## Practical Examples

### Blessed Tank Shell (Single Use, vs Moran)

| Blessing | Base | Bane (÷3) | Temp (×15%) | Final |
|----------|------|-----------|-------------|-------|
| Puissance +1 | 25 | 8.3 | 1.25 | **2 energy** |
| Puissance +2 | 100 | 33 | 5 | **5 energy** |
| Accuracy +1 | 25 | 8.3 | 1.25 | **2 energy** |
| Accuracy +2 | 100 | 33 | 5 | **5 energy** |

**Time:** 1 hour for any of these

### Permanent Tank Gun Blessing (vs Moran)

| Blessing | Base | Bane (÷3) | Final |
|----------|------|-----------|-------|
| Accuracy +1 | 250 | 83 | **83 energy** |
| Accuracy +2 | 1,000 | 333 | **333 energy** |
| Puissance +1 | 250 | 83 | **83 energy** |

**Time:** 1 hour (Accuracy +1) to 4 hours (Accuracy +2)

## Special Note: Meaningful Sacrifices

*Sometimes a treasured possession, a trophy of war, or something with emotional weight may be accepted by Ilpatar. The GM determines if such a sacrifice is meaningful enough to provide energy beyond its mere monetary value.*
